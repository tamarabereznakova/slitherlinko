What is a Slitherlink?

Slitherlink is a logic game in which the player's (your) task is to draw a single closed loop.
CAN YOU BEAT TOP 10 !?

Rules

× Tiles with a number MUST have exactly as many active edges as the number they contain.

× A tile without a number can have any number of active edges.

× Try to create one single closed loop

Controls

× Click on the edge between two points to activate it (green).

× Clicking the edge a second time marks it as empty (red).

× Clicking the edge a third time deactivates it completely (no color).


× The game is automatically evaluated after each move.

× WARNING, the TIMER starts with you first move

TOP 10

× Scoring - the fewer moves and shorter time, the better the score.

× The top 10 players are displayed separately for each level.

× After finishing the game, you can always decide whether you want to record your game in the leaderboard (if you made it there).

Tips that might be useful to you:

corner points can have 0 or 2 active edges coming out of them,

no tile will ever have all 4 of its edges active
